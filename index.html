About

Version 2.3

Glass Notes

A calm, frosted-glass notebook

Glass Notes is a soft, quiet place to write. Everything about it is designed to feel light and calm — floating glass panels, gentle motion, soft color, and nothing that gets in the way of your thoughts.

Under the surface it stays private by default, speaks five languages, and works completely offline. No accounts to create, no servers we control, no trackers, no ads — just you, your words, and a little bit of glass.

Updates

Version 2.3 · What's new
  • 5 languages. English, Arabic, French, Spanish and Chinese — with full RTL support for Arabic.
  • Button sounds. Five soft, natural sounds for taps, opens, long-presses, success and errors.
  • Smoothness engine. Rebuilt motion system with GPU-friendly layers — same look, half the RAM.
  • Default radius is 40px. Softer, calmer shapes everywhere — adjustable any time.
  • My Work section. Beautiful cards for the other projects, with one tap to open.
  • Site protection. Login rate limiting and safer copy behaviour.
