About

The story, the features, and the people behind Glass Notes.

Version 2.0

Glass Notes

A calm, frosted-glass notebook

Glass Notes is a soft, quiet place to write. Everything about it is designed to feel light and calm — floating glass panels, gentle motion, soft color, and nothing that gets in the way of your thoughts. It works like a notebook should: you open it, you write, and everything is saved.

Under the surface, it is a private, offline notebook. Your notes live only on your own device — no cloud, no server, no tracking, no ads. Just you, your words, and a little bit of glass.

Updates

Version 2.0 · What's new

The 2.0 Update

Pinned notes, download & share, and a smoother editor

  • Pin up to 3 notes. Long-press any note card and tap Pin to keep it at the top of your Home.
  • Download any note. Save a note as a clean .txt file straight from the long-press menu.
  • Share instantly. Send a note through your device's share sheet, or copy it to the clipboard with one tap.
  • Sign in from Phone or Computer. The login screen now asks where you are signing in from, and links your devices to your account.
  • Connected Devices panel. See your linked devices in Settings — with a green check for the other device when it's synced.
  • Taller, scrollable format panel. All your text tools now live in a slim glass panel that scrolls smoothly.
  • Small polish everywhere. Softer motion, tighter spacing, and a calmer glass look across the whole app.

How it works

Everything stays on your device

Notes are saved in your browser's local storage — never uploaded.

Works fully offline

No internet needed. Open it anywhere, anytime.

Saves as you write

Notes auto-save on close, on tab switch, or when you tap Save.

Private sign-in

Passwords are hashed and stored on-device — no server ever sees them.

What's inside

Rich text editor

Bold, italic, underline, lists, colors, and clickable links.

Typography controls

Font, size, weight, spacing, alignment and zoom for every note.

Themes & accents

Light, dark, or automatic — with ten accent colors to choose from.

Search & Trash

Find any note instantly. Deleted notes wait safely in the Trash.

Export & backup

Download all your notes as a single .json file any time.

Glass design

Adjustable blur, corner radius, and floating color orbs behind everything.

Pin, download & share

Keep the important ones on top, or save & share any note instantly.
